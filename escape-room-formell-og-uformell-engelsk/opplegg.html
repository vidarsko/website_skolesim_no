📘 Quick language guide

Formal: precise wording, complete forms, no slang. Used in reports and official writing.

Semi-formal: polite and natural, often suitable for colleagues and professional emails.

Informal: relaxed wording, contractions and slang may be fine with friends.

Remember: Formal is not always better. Match audience, purpose and genre.
